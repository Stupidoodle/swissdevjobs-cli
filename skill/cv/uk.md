# CV conventions — United Kingdom (`uk`)

Applies to: devitjobs-uk.

## Format

- **2 pages is the norm** ("CV", never "resume" in writing). One page is
  fine for juniors; three is pushing it.
- **No photo. Ever.** A photo on a UK CV reads as a mistake and creates
  discrimination-liability discomfort for the employer.
- No date of birth, no marital status, no nationality line — same reason.
- A short personal statement / profile (2–3 lines) at the top is
  conventional and worth writing well; it is the most-read part.

## Content expectations

- **Right to work**: state it plainly ("Right to work in the UK" or the
  visa route) — post-Brexit this is the first filter for EU candidates.
  If the posting says visa sponsorship is available and the user needs it,
  say what is needed (Skilled Worker sponsorship) rather than hiding it.
- Achievements over duties: UK recruiters expect bullet points with
  outcomes and numbers, not responsibility lists.
- References: "References available on request" is the convention; never
  list referee contact details on the CV itself.

## Language rules

- English only. British spelling (organise, analyse) for UK-headquartered
  companies; not worth flagging otherwise.

## Cover letter

- Shorter and less ceremonial than DACH: 3–4 paragraphs, direct, tailored.
  Many UK tech applications skip it entirely — write one when the posting
  asks or when the ATS has a field for it, keep it under 300 words.
- "Dear Hiring Team" acceptable; a real name better.
