# CV conventions — Netherlands (`nl`)

Applies to: devitjobs-nl.

## Format

- **1–2 pages, direct and unadorned.** Dutch business culture prizes
  brevity and plain statements; a padded CV reads worse than a short one.
- Photo optional and genuinely split — common enough not to be a mistake,
  never required. Skip it when unsure.
- Date of birth still appears on many Dutch CVs; safe to include, safe to
  drop.

## Content expectations

- **Work authorization**: EU citizens state citizenship; non-EU state the
  permit or that the employer is a recognized sponsor route (highly
  skilled migrant / kennismigrant). Dutch tech sponsors a lot — say what
  is needed instead of hiding it.
- English is the working language of most Dutch tech postings — an
  English CV is fine unless the posting itself is in Dutch.
- Dutch level is worth one honest line even for English roles (A2 "learning"
  included); it signals intent to integrate.
- Hobbies/personal interests appear on Dutch CVs more than elsewhere; one
  line is culturally normal, not filler.

## Language rules

- Dutch posting → Dutch documents if the user's Dutch is genuinely up to
  it; otherwise apply in English and say so — Dutch employers prefer
  honest English over machine-translated Dutch.
- English posting → English.

## Motivation letter

- Expected more often than in the UK/US, but short: half a page, direct
  ("Ik solliciteer…" / straight to fit), no ceremony. Directness is
  politeness here.
