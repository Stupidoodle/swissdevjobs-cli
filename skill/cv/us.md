# CV conventions — US & Canada (`us`)

Applies to: devitjobs-us (devitjobs.com lists both countries).

## Format

- **One page** ("resume") for under ~10 years of experience; two pages only
  when genuinely earned. This is the strictest length culture of any board
  in the tool — the long-form DACH CV must be cut hard, not translated.
- **No photo, no date of birth, no nationality, no marital status.**
  US employers may discard photographed resumes outright to avoid
  discrimination exposure.
- ATS-safe layout matters more than in Europe: single column is safest,
  standard section headers, no tables or graphics that parsers mangle —
  most US applications are machine-read before a human sees them.

## Content expectations

- **Work authorization**: "Authorized to work in the US" / "Requires H-1B
  sponsorship" — state it when the posting asks or sponsorship is needed;
  Canadian postings likewise (work permit / PR status).
- Impact bullets with numbers ("cut deploy time 40%") are the house style.
  Duty lists read as filler.
- Education goes below experience for anyone non-junior; GPA only if
  recent and strong.
- References: omit entirely — not even "on request".

## Language rules

- English. American spelling for US companies; Canadian postings tolerate
  either.

## Cover letter

- Mostly optional in US tech; write one only when the posting asks or the
  company is clearly letter-reading (non-profit, government, traditional).
  When written: short, specific, zero flattery.
